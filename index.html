This policy explains what data Pourfect: Liquid Sort Puzzle ("the App") collects and how it is used.

Summary

  • No account or login is required.
  • Your level progress is stored only on your device.
  • The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device data as described below.

Data stored on your device

The App saves your current level number in its private storage on your device. It never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to show interstitial and optional rewarded ads. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (device identifier for ads)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device information (model, OS version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks) and diagnostics

This data is used to deliver, measure and, where you have consented, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Data is encrypted in transit.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent through Google's consent form when the App starts. You can reset or opt out of personalized ads anytime in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
